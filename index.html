About Us

Our Guiding Principles

We are a company founded on the principles of ethics, trust, and family values, striving to make transportation not just a service, but a positive experience for everyone. Inspired by a family legacy that has always championed care, integrity, and community support, we aim to bring these values to modern transportation.

Our mission is simple yet profound: to provide safe, reliable, and ethical transportation services that you can depend on every day. Safety is at the heart of everything we do. That's why we go the extra mile to rigorously vet our drivers through comprehensive background checks, professional training, and continuous evaluations. When you choose us, you can travel with confidence, knowing that your well-being is our top priority.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the product and billing. Can't find the answer you're looking for? Please chat to our friendly team.

Is there a free trial available?

Yes, you can try us for free for 30 days. If you want, we'll provide you with a free, personalized 30-minute onboarding call to get you up and running as soon as possible.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time.

What is your cancellation policy?

You can cancel anytime. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period.

Can other info be added to an invoice?

Yes, you can add your company name, address and tax details to every invoice.

How does billing work?

Billing is monthly. You will get an invoice by email after each payment.

How do I change my account email?

Go to account settings, choose "Email" and enter your new address.
